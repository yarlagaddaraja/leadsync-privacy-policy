# Privacy Policy – LeadSync Pro

**Last updated: November 18, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
LeadSync Pro is a learning and demonstration project designed to retrieve and manage customer enquiries submitted through Meta Lead Ads.

## 2. Information We Collect
When you submit a lead form, we may collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information to:
- Respond to your enquiries.
- Manage and track customer leads.
- Demonstrate lead management functionality.

## 4. Data Sharing
Lead information may be retrieved from Meta platforms and processed by the LeadSync Pro application. We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by this application.

## 6. Data Retention
Information is retained only as needed for the project's lead management and demonstration purposes.
